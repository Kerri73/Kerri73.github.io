Our Story

Meet the Humans Behind the Squad

“They scoop. I supervise.” — Paws McClean

Less time scooping. More time enjoying life with your dogs.

Backyard Bomb Squad was created from a simple idea: helping people spend less time worrying about pet waste and more time enjoying life with their dogs.

As passionate animal lovers, we know pets are family. We also know that cleaning up the backyard is one of the least enjoyable parts of pet ownership. Between busy schedules, work, and everyday life, it’s a chore that’s easy to put off.

That’s why we started Backyard Bomb Squad—to create cleaner, safer, and more enjoyable outdoor spaces for families and their furry friends. A clean yard means more time to play, relax, entertain, and make memories without watching every step.

We’re proud to do the dirty work so you don’t have to, giving you more time to enjoy the moments that matter most with your pets.

Kerri & Joel
